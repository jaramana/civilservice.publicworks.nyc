About

NYC Civil Service Exams brings exam schedules, civil service lists and title salaries into one place.

Why

The City publishes exam schedules, civil service lists and title salaries separately. This site brings them together so readers can check application periods and published pay in one place.

Scope

The site shows published exam dates, active lists and title salaries. Open, coming-soon and closed labels are calculated from application dates. No numbers are estimated. Missing fields are left out. Confirm a date on NYC.gov before relying on it. Data lists the sources, limits and downloads.

Built

Python, pandas and requests prepare the data and calendar files. The website uses static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, served from GitHub Pages.

Independence

No agency reviewed this site.

Credits

Data from NYC Open Data and the Department of Citywide Administrative Services (DCAS) exam pages. Median pay context comes from The Pay Gap. Claude was used in development.

Reuse
